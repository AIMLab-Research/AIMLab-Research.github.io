AIM Lab

AI and Multimodal Learning Lab

School of Computing, Utah State University

The AIM Lab at Utah State University conducts research in artificial intelligence, computer vision, multimodal learning, vision-language models, and robust visual intelligence. Our goal is to develop reliable, efficient, and generalizable AI systems for real-world applications.
