Research

AIM Lab focuses on artificial intelligence and multimodal learning, with emphasis on computer vision, vision-language models, multimodal large language models, and robust adaptation under real-world distribution shifts.

Research Directions

  • Vision-language models and multimodal large language models
  • Robust and generalizable visual intelligence
  • Efficient adaptation and parameter-efficient fine-tuning
  • Industrial anomaly understanding and visual anomaly detection
  • Trustworthy AI under distribution shifts
  • Applications in remote sensing, healthcare, and biometrics
